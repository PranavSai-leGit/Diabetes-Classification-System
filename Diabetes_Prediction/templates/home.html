About Diabetes

Common symptoms include:
  • Frequent urination (polyuria)
  • Increased thirst (polydipsia) and hunger
  • Extreme fatigue and blurry vision
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Slow-healing sores or cuts
Key preventative steps:
  • Engage in 150+ minutes of moderate weekly exercise
  • Eat a nutrient-dense diet low in sugar and refined carbs
  • Maintain a healthy weight and BMI
  • Stay well hydrated throughout the day
  • Get regular blood screenings for early detection
Primary risk factors:
  • Family history or genetics
  • Sedentary lifestyle (lack of regular exercise)
  • Age (risk increases over 45)
  • High blood pressure or cholesterol
  • History of gestational diabetes during pregnancy

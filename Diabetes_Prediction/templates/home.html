About This Project

This Diabetes Prediction Model utilizes machine learning to assess the risk of developing diabetes based on key health indicators.

By analyzing vital signs such as BMI, HbA1c levels, and blood glucose, this tool aims to provide early risk detection, empowering individuals and clinicians to take preventative action before symptoms escalate.
